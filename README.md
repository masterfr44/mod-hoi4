## English

### Introduction

Welcome to the HOI4 project!

This mod is inspired by the game *Iron Order 1919* and aims to bring that universe to life in Hearts of Iron IV.

The mod is open to everyone and free to modify. The only request is that you credit me in any modifications.

### Goals

- Bring to life a universe I admire.
- Create a new style of gameplay.
- Create new designs for countries and units.
